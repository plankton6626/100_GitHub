# Landau study notes: tensors and relativistic kinematics

[中文](README.md) · [English](README.en.md) · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Version 0.7, 2026-10-20. Topics: mixed tensors, the Minkowski metric and its inverse, delta/epsilon and duality, polar and axial vectors, four-dimensional differentiation and integration, four-velocity and four-acceleration. Basic linear algebra, calculus and special relativity are assumed.

The author supplied handwritten derivations and two Word descriptions. AI assisted with transcription, organization, translation and LaTeX typesetting. The author revised the notes repeatedly and manually edited the Chinese source. All editions were compiled and visually checked; this does not imply independent verification of every mathematical claim.

## Source material

[Ten manuscript images](manuscript/) preserve the handwriting, with the upper screen background cropped and image metadata removed. Private originals remain local. [Two Word source copies](sources/) retain the text, with author and last-editor core properties cleared. The original manuscripts mention generalized Stokes; the electronic editions follow the author's later revision and discuss each type of integral separately.

Reference: the Chinese eighth edition of The Classical Theory of Fields, ISBN 978-7-04-035173-6, mainly sections 6–7. Eleven book photographs numbered 01 and 04 remain local and are excluded from this directory; citation is not permission to reproduce whole pages.
