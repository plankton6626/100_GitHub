# 100_GitHub · Open Study Notes

[中文](README.md) · [English](README.en.md) · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Personal mathematics and physics study notes, including explanations of concepts, derivations, and supporting background. The aim is to make the notes understandable on their own, traceable to their sources, and open to improvement through reader feedback.

Documents normally have separate Chinese, English, and Japanese editions, with readable PDFs and editable LaTeX sources. The three editions share the main content, notation, sections, and equation numbering; pagination and line breaks may differ.

These are personal study materials, not official solutions from a course or institution. Check the assumptions, scope, and revision version stated in each document.

## Feedback and revisions

Feedback on incorrect formulas, missing assumptions, inconsistent notation, unclear steps, and translation issues is welcome.

- **Issues**: Report a specific error or a concrete improvement. Include the file or title, language edition, revision version, section, equation number or short quotation, and an explanation. Add a suggested correction or reference source if available.
- **Discussions**: Discuss questions about understanding, alternative derivations, or study suggestions. Use this feature once enabled; until then, questions may be posted as Issues.
- **Pull requests**: Suggest changes to sources, translations, or formatting. Explain the reason for the change and, where possible, check corresponding content in all three language editions. For LaTeX changes, indicate whether compilation was verified.

Once the repository is published on GitHub, these feedback channels can be used from its page. Verified issues will be addressed in subsequent revisions. Do not include student IDs, private contact details, course materials without permission to publish, or other personal information in your report.

## References

Each document lists the sources actually used and identifies sources alongside relevant claims, quotations, or figures. Unverified bibliographic details, page numbers, and DOIs are not presented as confirmed references. Attribution and permission to reproduce material are checked separately.
